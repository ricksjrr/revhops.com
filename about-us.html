RevHops is a revenue operations consultancy in Phoenix, Arizona. Small on purpose, deep in one thing, and straight with you about the parts that will be difficult.

Started by someone who had to fix it himself

I am James. Before RevHops I spent [00] years inside revenue teams rather than beside them — running the systems, owning the number, and being the person who had to explain to the board why the forecast and the invoices disagreed.

That is the reason this shop exists. The agencies I hired were good at building what I asked for and bad at telling me when I had asked for the wrong thing. The people who could tell me that were expensive, busy, and gone by month three.

So RevHops takes fewer clients and keeps the same people on them. It is a less scalable business. It is a much better one to be a client of.

How we work

Every engagement runs the same way regardless of size: agree what good looks like, write it down, build against the document, hand it over with the documentation. The parts that go wrong are almost always the parts nobody wrote down.

We are also willing to be the wrong answer. If the job needs five bodies on site next week, or the real problem is a hiring problem, we would rather say that on the first call than find out together in month four.
