Platinum is a real bar, and it is not the top one.

Anyone whose pitch is their partner tier is selling you their partner tier. Here is what it is actually worth knowing.

HubSpot ranks partners on how much software they sell and how well the customers who buy it do afterwards. Platinum is the third tier of five.

What it is good for: the certifications are current, the portals we have built are still in use, and we have a channel into HubSpot when something is broken on their side rather than ours. That last one saves more time than the badge does.

What it is not: a reason to buy HubSpot. If you own Salesforce and it is working, we will say so and you will have saved a migration.
