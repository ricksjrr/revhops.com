What Platinum actually means

HubSpot ranks its partners on how much software they sell and how well the customers who buy it do afterwards. Platinum is the third tier of five. It is a real bar and it is not the top one, and anyone who tells you their tier is the whole story is selling you their tier.

What it is useful for: it means the certifications are current, the portals we have built are still in use, and we have a channel into HubSpot when something is broken on their side rather than ours. That last one saves more time than the badge does.

What it is not: a reason to buy HubSpot. If you already own Salesforce and it is working, we will say so.
