Which plan you actually need

Four tiers and five add-ons. Most teams are sold more than they use, so here is the short version.

  • Lite is a pipeline, contacts and email sync. Enough for a small team getting deals out of a spreadsheet, and no further.
  • Growth adds workflow automation, products and the document tooling. Most teams land here and stay.
  • Premium adds team management, projects and the reporting depth a sales manager asks for by month three.
  • Ultimate adds permission sets, security controls and the higher automation ceilings. Worth it above roughly 25 seats, rarely below.
  • The add-ons, LeadBooster, Campaigns, Projects, Web Visitors and Smart Docs, are priced on top. We will tell you which two you need and which three you do not.
