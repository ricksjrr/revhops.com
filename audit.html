Request your audit

Tell us which portal and what is bothering you. If something is actually on fire, say so and we will look at that first.

What we look at

Six passes through the portal, in this order, because each one changes what the next one means.

Data and objects

Contacts, companies, deals and anything custom. Duplicates, properties nobody has filled in since the year they were made, and the fields your reporting quietly depends on.

Pipelines and lifecycle

Whether the stages match how you actually sell, and whether a lifecycle stage means the same thing to marketing, to sales and to whoever built the dashboard.

Automation

Every live workflow, what fires it, and which two are fighting each other over the same property. Usually the fastest thing to fix and the last thing anyone looks at.

Reporting

The dashboards people open, the ones they do not, and whether the number at the top of them is one you could defend in a board meeting.

Integrations and sync

What writes into the portal, what reads out of it, and the place a record gets overwritten every night by something upstream that nobody owns.

Access and adoption

Who is in the portal, what they can see, and the spreadsheet your team invented to get around the bit that does not work.

What comes back

01

One written page

Not a sixty-slide deck and not a call you have to sit through to hear the findings. One page, in plain words, that you can forward to the person who owns the budget.

02

Three things, ranked

Everything we find is ordered by what it costs you to leave it, with an honest guess at the effort beside each one. The top three are the ones worth doing this quarter.

03
